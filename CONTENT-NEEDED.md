# Your portfolio content

Send rough notes; polished copy is not necessary. Anything optional can be left out.

- Name and professional title.
- Main audience: employers, freelance clients, or both.
- Short bio: what you do, what you enjoy, and what makes your approach different.
- Location and availability (optional).
- Portrait photo (optional).
- Skills, tools, and technologies.
- Two to six projects: name, description, your role, tools, results if known, live/repository links, and screenshots.
- Experience: roles, organisations, dates, responsibilities, and achievements.
- Education or certifications (optional).
- Contact email and social links, such as LinkedIn and GitHub.
- CV PDF and booking link (optional).
- Preferred colours and anything you particularly like about meetzainab.com.
- Testimonials only if you have real quotes and permission to display them (optional).

The first draft is in dist/index.html and dist/style.css. Text in square brackets is waiting for your content. Illustrative project artwork and the portrait silhouette are placeholders, not claims about your work. Contact links and a CV download can be added when the real destinations/files are provided.
